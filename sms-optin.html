Columbia Cue Club — Match Alert SMS

How players opt in, what we send, and how to stop. Last updated: 2026‑09‑05.

What this program is

Columbia Cue Club Match Alerts is a transactional (non‑marketing) text‑message service operated by Kevin Long (DBA CleverClever) for players competing in Columbia Cue Club pool tournaments. When it is your turn to play and your match has been assigned to a table, we send you a one‑time SMS so you can come to the table promptly. We do not send advertising, promotions, or marketing of any kind through this program.

Message content

A match‑ready alert looks like this:

"Your match against Kevin Long is starting now on table 6."

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You receive a text only when one of your matches is called to a table during an event you have entered — typically a small number of messages per tournament.

How you opt in (verbal consent)

Consent is collected verbally, in person, at the tournament check‑in / bracket desk. When you register for an event, a staff member asks whether you would like to receive match‑ready text alerts and, if you say yes, records your mobile number and marks your entry as opted‑in. A typical script:

Staff: "Would you like to get a text message when your match is up and assigned to a table, so you don't miss your call? Message frequency varies, message & data rates may apply, reply STOP to opt out, and reply HELP for help. May I add your mobile number for match alerts?"

Player: "Yes." — Staff confirms the number back to the player and enrolls them; the player's first match alert also serves to confirm enrollment. A "no" is recorded and no messages are sent.

Only players who explicitly say yes are enrolled. We never buy, rent, or import phone numbers, and a player's mobile number is used solely to deliver their own match alerts.

Getting help & opting out

  • Reply HELP to any message for help.
  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • You may also ask any Columbia Cue Club staff member to remove your number.

Costs

Columbia Cue Club does not charge for match alerts. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.

Privacy & terms

See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions. We do not share mobile opt‑in data or consent with third parties except the messaging provider that delivers the texts on our behalf.
