Privacy Policy

Columbia Cue Club tournament system & Match Alerts. Last updated: 2026‑09‑05.

Who we are

Columbia Cue Club operates a tournament‑management application at codeonline.io/15ball for running pool tournaments (brackets, entrants, scoring) and an optional SMS match‑alert service for players.

What we collect

  • Tournament data: player display names, entrant/match state, and scores entered by staff to run events.
  • Mobile number (optional): only if you opt in to match alerts. We record your number and that you consented.
  • Organizer accounts: email address for staff/director sign‑in (passwordless magic link).

How we use it

  • To operate tournaments and display brackets, scores, and standings.
  • To send you a match‑ready SMS when your match is assigned to a table (only if you opted in).
  • To authenticate staff and directors.
SMS consent is never shared. Mobile information and consent obtained for match alerts are used solely to deliver those alerts and are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for their own use. The only third party involved is our messaging provider (Twilio), which transmits the messages on our behalf.

Retention

We keep tournament records for club history and results. You can ask us to delete your mobile number at any time by replying STOP to a message or contacting us; opting out removes you from future alerts.

Your choices

  • Opting in to SMS is voluntary and not required to play in any event.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help at any time.
  • Contact us to request access to or deletion of your information.

Security

Data is stored on access‑controlled servers. We limit access to staff who need it to run events.
