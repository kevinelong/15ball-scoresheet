Columbia Cue Club Match Alerts. Last updated: 2026‑09‑05.

1. The program

By providing your mobile number and opting in (verbally, at a Columbia Cue Club tournament check‑in desk), you agree to receive transactional SMS text messages from Columbia Cue Club notifying you that your tournament match is ready and assigned to a table. This is not a marketing program.

2. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You receive a message only when one of your matches is called to a table during an event you have entered.

3. Cost

Columbia Cue Club does not charge for these messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.

4. Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. After you send STOP, we will send one confirmation message and you will receive no further match alerts unless you opt in again. You may also ask any staff member to remove your number.

5. Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us using the details below.

6. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to your carrier's coverage and your device.

7. Privacy

Your mobile number is used only to deliver your match alerts and is handled per our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share mobile opt‑in information with third parties except the messaging provider that delivers the texts on our behalf.

We may update these terms; the current version is always posted at this URL.
